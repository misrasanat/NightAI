Step 2: Wire Up Mobile Microphone & Audio Streaming (Mobile to Backend)
Once the backend agents can actually perform actions from raw text, make the voice interaction real:

Mobile Audio Capture:

Replace the mocked toggle in VoiceScreen.jsx with expo-av or react-native-live-audio-stream.

WebSocket Streaming (/api/v1/stream):

Backend STT Processing:

Replace the hardcoded string in backend/app/services/stt.py with the actual Whisper API call (or Gemini native audio transcription) on the incoming stream bytes.

Pass the transcribed text into brain.py for routing.

Step 3: Complete the End-to-End Voice Feedback Loop
Close the loop so NightAI speaks back to you on your device:

Audio Synthesis:

Once an Agent executes its task, pass the result string to tts.py to generate speech via ElevenLabs.

Mobile Playback:

In VoiceScreen.jsx, take the incoming audio_url or base64 audio stream from the WebSocket and play it through expo-av Sound objects.
